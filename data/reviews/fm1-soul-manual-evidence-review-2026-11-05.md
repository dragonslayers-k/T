## What the Manual actually verifies

1. Unit-card beads represent summon conditions and level. The manual describes seven colored soul types and a blank, colorless bead.
2. At summon time, a colorless/blank bead may be paid with any attribute's soul.
3. Starting with the next preparation turn, the colorless amount becomes recurring upkeep for maintaining the unit.
4. Upkeep is paid from the soul regenerated by terrain. The manual's example: 10 terrain-generated souls minus 5 colorless upkeep leaves 5 usable souls.
5. If regenerated soul is insufficient for upkeep, the unpaid amount destroys the corresponding number of deck cards during the preparation step.
6. The manual separately says terrain generates one attribute soul per turn; this supports the general resource-generation model, not a hybrid-token payment rule.

## What the Manual does NOT verify

- It does not name numeric enum `82` as `NCS` or prove that `NCS` is identical to the manual's blank/colorless bead.
- It does not define numeric enum `83` / `BONUS`, its earning source, spending order, or whether it substitutes for ordinary color souls.
- It does not explain any of the 14 hybrid enum codes (e.g. `DARK_FIRE`) or whether a hybrid token requires both colors, either color, or a special combination.
- It does not specify ordering/selection when several units have upkeep and the player's regenerated soul is insufficient.
- Therefore none of those enum payment rules may be promoted from this manual alone.

## Decision
